# App Review Notes - WCS Platform v1.0 (12)

This build contains no subscription purchase flow, external subscription purchase flow, commerce flow, external commerce, pricing display, checkout path, restore control, product identifier, or paid digital-content offer inside the iOS app.

Reviewer path:

1. Launch the app and sign in with the provided reviewer account.
2. Open Profile -> Access.
3. Confirm access is described as organization-assigned account access only.
4. Open Programs and lessons. Unassigned lessons use neutral organization-assigned access messaging only.
5. Confirm there are no subscription buttons, payment links, checkout paths, pricing rows, paid digital-content offers, or restore controls.

Access model:

- Learner access is assigned by organization account configuration.
- The app does not include subscriptions, payment links, checkout, external commerce, pricing, or paid digital-content purchase prompts.
- Debug/test-only launch flags do not expose restricted content flows in Release.
